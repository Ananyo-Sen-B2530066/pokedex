    Help & Items What everything on the Pokédex means
    Pokéballs — the Pokédex's signature red-ball motif; it marks the app logo and the home screen hero.
    LIVE — the server is connected and the full Pokédex (1025 species + forms) is loaded and ready.
    Loading — the database cache is being fetched or rebuilt; the app answers only once this turns LIVE.
    Type icons — coloured chips showing a Pokémon's or a move's types; colours follow the official type-coloring.
    MegaGigantamaxAlolanForm tabs — some species have alternate forms (Base, Mega, Primal, Gigantamax, Regional). Each tab shows that form's own entry, types, and stats.
    ✨Shiny badge — the ✨ marks the alternate-coloured sprite. Tap popups show it next to the normal sprite.
    🔊Cry button — plays the Pokémon's cry sound from the games.
    PWR 90Move stats — Power (PWR), Accuracy (ACC), PP, and the generation a move debuted in.
    Move kinds — Z-Moves, Max Moves, and Gigantamax moves carry their own symbol.
    💥 ✨ 🛡Damage class — physical, special, or status moves.
    🧠Ability chips — click an ability to read its full description. Abilities marked Hidden are hidden abilities. Clicking anywhere outside the box closes it.
    HiddenHidden badge — the ability is a hidden ability (page header badge on the Abilities page).
    2×Type effectiveness — 0× immune, ¼×/½× resist, 2×/4× weak. Row colour hints the strength.
    🔄Evolution chart — shows the family tree. Regional families draw one track per region.
    📡 📷 🖼Scan — random Pokémon, your camera (real-world toys/figures), or screenshots from your gallery.
    🗂Filters & search — filter the list by type on the Pokémon page, or combine type, region, form, and generation on the Search page.
    ★Legendary mark — a gold ★ on the top-right of a card or popup marks a Legendary Pokémon.
    ✦Mythical mark — a rose ✦ on the top-right of a card or popup marks a Mythical Pokémon.
    🏡Home — tap the Pokéball logo anytime to return to the home screen.
